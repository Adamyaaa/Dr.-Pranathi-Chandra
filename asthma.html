Asthma Assessment & Management

What is Asthma?

Asthma is a chronic respiratory condition where the airways in the lungs become inflamed, narrow, and produce excess mucus. This makes it difficult for air to flow freely, leading to recurring episodes of wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing. Asthma symptoms are often triggered by specific environmental factors, stress, or exercise.

What happens at the first online consultation?

Because Dr. Pranathi Chandra practices 100% via telemedicine, your first consultation will take place over a secure Google Meet video call. She will take a detailed history of your symptoms, identify potential environmental triggers, and assess how frequently your asthma flares up. Importantly, she will ask you to demonstrate how you currently use your inhaler on camera, as improper inhaler technique is the leading cause of poorly controlled asthma.

Which tests are usually needed?

Before the call, it is highly recommended to have a recent Pulmonary Function Test (Spirometry) done at a local diagnostic centre. Dr. Pranathi will review these reports alongside any recent Chest X-rays. In some cases, specific blood panels for allergies (like Serum IgE or Absolute Eosinophil Count) may also be reviewed.
