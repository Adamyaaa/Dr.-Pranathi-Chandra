Common questions

Before your consultation

How soon will my appointment be confirmed?

The team checks the schedule and replies on WhatsApp, usually the same day during working hours. You will receive your Google Meet link in that message.

Do I need to pay anything to book?

No payment is taken on the website. The team will send you a secure payment link via WhatsApp when confirming your Google Meet slot.

What should I prepare for the video call?

Please keep any previous prescriptions, chest X-ray or CT films, recent blood reports, and the inhaler or nebuliser you are currently using nearby. Having the actual device matters — a large part of the first visit is checking how you are using it.

My cough has lasted more than three weeks. Is that serious?

A cough lasting over three weeks should be assessed rather than treated blindly with repeated antibiotics or cough syrups. There are several possible reasons, and the assessment usually starts with a chest X-ray and a sputum test. It is worth getting checked.

Do I have to visit a physical clinic, or can I consult online?

Both options are available! You can consult online via secure video call (9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, ₹400) or visit in person at Surya Diagnosis, Opp: Gandhi Hospital Back Gate, Secunderabad (5:30 PM to 8:30 PM, ₹500).

Is my information kept private?

Your name, number and the reason for the visit are used only to arrange and follow up your consultation. They are not sold or shared for marketing. You can ask for your details to be deleted at any time by messaging the clinic. See the privacy note.
