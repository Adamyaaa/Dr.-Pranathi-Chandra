Tuberculosis (TB) Care

What is Tuberculosis (TB)?

Tuberculosis is a serious, highly contagious bacterial infection that primarily attacks the lungs, though it can affect other parts of the body. Symptoms often include a persistent cough lasting more than three weeks, coughing up blood, unintended weight loss, fever, and severe night sweats. TB is entirely curable with a strict and sustained course of antibiotics.

What happens at the first online consultation?

Your Google Meet consultation will involve a thorough evaluation of your symptom timeline, any known contact with TB patients, and a review of your weight loss history. If you have been treated for TB in the past, Dr. Pranathi will assess the risk of drug-resistant TB (MDR-TB). You will receive clear, step-by-step guidance on how to take your medications, manage side effects, and prevent spreading the infection.

Which tests are usually needed?

Please have your Sputum AFB smear and CBNAAT/GeneXpert reports ready before the call, as these are the gold standard for diagnosing TB. A recent Chest X-ray is also vital. Baseline blood tests, including a Complete Blood Count (CBC), Liver Function Test (LFT), and ESR, are usually required before starting the heavy antibiotic regimen.
