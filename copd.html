Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

What is COPD?

COPD refers to a group of progressive lung diseases, most notably emphysema and chronic bronchitis. These conditions damage the air sacs and block airflow in the lungs, making it increasingly difficult to breathe. COPD is most commonly linked to long-term exposure to lung irritants, such as cigarette smoke, cooking smoke (biomass fuel), or industrial fumes.

What happens at the first online consultation?

During your online Google Meet consultation, Dr. Pranathi will carefully evaluate your level of breathlessness using standard scales, document your exposure history, and assess how the disease affects your daily life. The goal is to optimize your breathing function, reduce the frequency of severe flare-ups (exacerbations), and review your current daily medication routine.

Which tests are usually needed?

A recent Spirometry (preferably a post-bronchodilator test) is essential to confirm the diagnosis and severity of COPD. You should also have a recent Chest X-ray or HRCT Thorax ready to upload or share during the call. Depending on your symptoms, a Complete Blood Count (CBC) or an Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) report may also be reviewed.
