How your information is handled

Short version: your details are used to arrange and follow up your consultation, and nothing else. They are not sold, and they are not used for marketing.

What is collected

  • Your name and mobile number
  • Your preferred day and session
  • Whatever you choose to write about the reason for the visit

You do not have to use the symptom note tool, and you do not have to write anything in the notes box. A name and number are enough to arrange an appointment.

What it is used for

  • Confirming, rescheduling or cancelling your appointment
  • Sending you the date, time and clinic address
  • Reminding you before the appointment
  • Clinical records once you become a patient, which are kept as required for medical records

How it is sent

The appointment request is sent as a WhatsApp message from your own phone to the clinic. It is delivered through WhatsApp, which applies its own terms and encryption. The clinic replies on the same number.

Who else sees it

Only the doctor and the clinic staff involved in arranging your appointment. Your information is not shared with third parties for advertising, is not sold, and is not passed to any listing or aggregator platform.

Your choices

  • You may ask for your details to be deleted at any time by messaging or calling the clinic
  • You may withdraw consent to be contacted at any time
  • You may ask what information is held about you
  • Clinical records, once created, are retained for the period required of medical records and cannot be deleted on request
